FAQ

Straight answers.

What exactly is PayRam? +
PayRam is payments infrastructure built for crypto operators — covering payment collection, payouts, wallets, SmartSweep, onramp, and merchant analytics. RampStack deploys and manages your dedicated PayRam environment.
Do you hold merchant funds? +
Never. PayRam runs on non-custodial rails. Funds sit in self-custodied wallets. RampStack has zero access.
What does managed actually mean? +
We deploy, monitor, upgrade, and operate your PayRam environment. You log into a dashboard. We get paged if something breaks.
Can we really go live in 6 hours? +
Yes. That's the median across all operators. The record is faster.
How is this different from Stripe Connect? +
Stripe is fiat-only. PayRam is built for crypto-native operators: multi-chain, unique deposit wallets, SmartSweep, card-to-crypto onramp. Entirely different category.
What if we want to self-host PayRam later? +
Your data and merchants are yours. Full export on request. We support migration to a self-hosted setup. No lock-in.
Do we need engineers to operate this? +
No. The PayRam dashboard is built for operators. API access is there when you want it.
Which chains are supported? +
Ethereum, Polygon, Tron, BSC, and more. Tell us what you need on the onboarding call.
